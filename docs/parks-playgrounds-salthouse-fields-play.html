About this place

The play areas at Salthouse Fields give a Clevedon visit a natural starting point for children. There is space to pause on the grass between play sessions, with the seafront offering a change of scene afterwards.

Our family idea: make the playground the main event and leave the rest of the afternoon open. If the model railway is operating, decide together whether to add a ride; it is a separate paid activity.

Practical information

Best for
Families who want playtime and a relaxed seaside setting.
Facilities
Children's play areas, skate park and nearby seafront amenities.
Parking
Use signed Salthouse Fields or seafront parking and check charges.
Good to know
Playground entry is free. Check equipment suitability for each child and supervise them near the adjoining roads and water.
