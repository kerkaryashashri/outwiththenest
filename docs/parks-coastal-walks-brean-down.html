About this place

Brean Down is a substantial coastal adventure rather than a flat seafront stroll. The headland reaches into the Bristol Channel, and its fort and open landscape give older children a destination to work towards.

Plan from the National Trust trail guide, taking account of the climb and your family's walking experience. Our family idea: bring binoculars and use a sheltered stop to compare the coast on either side of the Down.

Practical information

Best for
Older children and experienced family walkers.
Facilities
Visitor facilities at the base of the Down; little shelter on the headland.
Parking
Use the signed Brean Down parking area and check current charges.
Good to know
Expect steep steps or an uphill approach, exposed weather and dangerous cliffs. Keep children well back from edges and avoid tidal mud on the beach.
